Why MindSpace? Meet the Developer

OUR WHY

Many individuals (including myself) sometimes get lost in their thoughts and “what-ifs.”

This can lead to unnecessary stress and anxiety, which can cause harm to a person’s long-term health.

We believe that everyone deserves their own space to ease and reset their minds.

THE DEVELOPER

TIFFANY DINH

Hello! My name is Tiffany and I am a third-year Computer Science major at Cal Poly Pomona. I am passionate about web development, UI/UX, and human computer interaction.

I personally struggle a lot with anxiety/overthinking, which is what lead me to create this website. My mission is to help other individuals who are also struggling with these problems.
