-Activities-

Here are some activities to clear your mind!

Indoor Activities Outdoor Activities

GAMES

Video and board games are both ways to increase positive emotions and takes your mind off of what is bothering you.

COOKING/BAKING

Cooking and baking allows you to complete tasks at your own pace and activates mindfulness. It creates a sense of independence and boosts self-esteem.

EXERCISING

Exercising provides a healthy way to reduce stress. It also lowers stress levels and distracts you from negative thoughts.

HIKING

Hiking forces you to get out of the house and causes your brain to rest due to the outdoor environment.

BEACH

The beach has a peaceful/calming atmosphere and significantly reduces anxiety and stress. You can also swim, which is a soothing type of exercise.
